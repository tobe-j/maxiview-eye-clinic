Our Services

Comprehensive Eye Care & Outreach Services

From routine vision assessments and designer optical dispensing to advanced diagnostics, surgical eye treatment, and community outreach programs.

Book Service Consultation
01

Basic and Comprehensive Eye Care Services

Thorough routine eye health checkups, visual acuity testing, binocular vision evaluation, and preventive eye screenings for children, adults, and seniors.

  • Complete primary eye health assessments
  • Pediatric, adult & senior vision exams
02

Optical Services

Expert prescription of glasses, UV-protective sunglasses, contact lenses, personalized frame selection, and precision optical fitting tailored to your face and lifestyle.

  • Prescription glasses, sunglasses & contact lenses
  • Professional frame selection & custom fitting
03

Advanced Investigative and Diagnostic Services

High-precision computerized ocular diagnostics, intraocular pressure profiling, anterior segment biomicroscopy, and detailed retinal evaluations.

  • Computerized refraction & eye pressure checks
  • Early detection of glaucoma & cataracts
04

Medical Eye Care and Treatment

Targeted medical management for glaucoma, eye infections, conjunctivitis, dry eye syndrome, Harmattan allergies, diabetic eye care, and ocular injuries.

  • Glaucoma, allergy & dry eye therapy
  • Urgent ocular infection & foreign body care
05

Surgical Eye Treatment

Specialized surgical ophthalmic procedures, cataract extraction coordination, pterygium management, and structured pre- and post-operative recovery care.

  • Cataract & pterygium surgical management
  • Dedicated post-operative visual rehabilitation
06

Outreach Programs to Schools, Communities & Corporate Organisations

Mobile vision screening camps, school eye health initiatives, community blindness-prevention drives, and on-site corporate staff eye checkups.

  • School & community vision screening camps
  • On-site corporate organization wellness days
